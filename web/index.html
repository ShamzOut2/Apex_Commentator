    Photo booth

    Photographs each car loaded into the session in iRacing and uses it as the driver's car photo in the grid walk, saved to the active league. Needs iRacing's Video and Screen Capture setting on. It takes over the camera for about 2 seconds per car, so run it before you go live.

    Saved leagues

      Classes

      Each class gets its own colour and header in the driver list. Drivers join a class by their iRacing car class (the "iRacing classes" field) or by picking it in the roster below. A logo's background is recoloured to the class colour.

      Teams

      Team names, logos and colours, and which drivers are in each team, are set in the team builder. The roster's Team column is the same thing.

      Season calendar

      The season's rounds (track, layout, date, time, length) are set up with the championship; the standings, results and calendar board all come from them. If the league has made its own calendar graphic, add it here and the calendar board can show it instead.

      League logo

      Shown in the header of the full-screen boards (each board can turn it off).

      League banners

      Logos and banners for the full-screen boards (championship, calendar, results). Upload a wide one for across the top or bottom, and a tall one for the side; each board picks which to show.

      Drivers

      Nicknames, teams and notes show next to drivers in the overlay. Drivers are matched by iRacing customer ID, or by car number when there is no ID.

      DriverCust IDCar #Driver #NicknameTeamNotesClassCarPhotos

      Teams

      Teams show on the standings overlay, the lap tracker and the grid walk. The short name is used where space is tight; the colour is optional (the driver's class colour is used without one). Changes are saved with the league.

      Overlays

      Each has two versions. The overlay goes over the race in OBS (Browser Source at 1920×1080, transparent background). The full screen version is a complete screen of its own, with a background and every driver, to cut to on stream or show on a monitor (Open, then F11). Changes here show straight away.

      Pick a league above, or build one in the League builder first.

      Add the season's rounds. After a race, open its round and press Import from session (while still in the session) to fill in the results. Who finished (took the chequered flag), retired (DNF) or never started (DNS) is worked out from watching the race. Anyone who covered less than the minimum share of the race (Points & rules) is a DNF and scores nothing. Correct anything by hand: positions, laps led, fastest laps, status, or extra points (+ or −). During a race the live standings count it as the round marked live (by default the first round without results).

      Post-race penalties. Points come off the driver's championship total (and their team's) and are never dropped. Licence points are a running tally per driver, shown in the standings.

      DriverRoundPoints offLicence ptsReason

      Camera hotkeys

      Click a camera, then press the key you want for it. Backspace clears it. L, R and the arrow keys are taken (live, rewind, car ahead/behind).
