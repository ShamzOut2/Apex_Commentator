    Car pictures

    Point the app at the folder of car pictures you've made for the grid walk. Name each picture iRacingID.Carname, e.g. 123456.Porsche 911 GT3 R (992).png (PNG, JPG or WebP; a see-through background looks best, the car pointing right). Each driver is matched by their iRacing ID and the car they're in, so the right car and livery show for the right person; a driver with only one picture gets it whatever the car. New pictures dropped in the folder show up by themselves.

    Hotkeys

    Click something, then press the key you want for it. Backspace clears it. A key does one thing: giving it to something else takes it off the first. L, R and the arrow keys are taken (live, rewind, car ahead/behind). Plain keys work when an Apex Commentator window is in front; keys pressed with Ctrl or Alt (and Shift if you like), e.g. Ctrl+Alt+1, work whichever program is in front, iRacing and OBS included (🌐).

      Main classes are what the overlays show: each gets its own colour and header. Drivers join one by their iRacing car class (the "iRacing classes" field), by a class tag in front of their name, or by picking it in the Drivers list. A logo's background is recoloured to the class colour.

        Championship

        Unsaved changes
        Pick a league above, or build one in the League builder first.

        Add the season's rounds. With the box above ticked, each race's results are saved to its round when the race is over, so the standings are up to date straight away. Or, after a race, open its round and press Import from session (while still in the session) to fill in the results. Who finished (took the chequered flag), retired (DNF) or never started (DNS) is worked out from watching the race. Anyone who covered less than the minimum share of the race (Points & rules) is a DNF and scores nothing. Correct anything by hand: positions, laps led, fastest laps, status, or extra points (+ or −). During a race the live standings count it as the round marked live (by default the first round without results).

        Build the rounds from a calendar

        Fill in the season in one go: by scanning the league's calendar graphic, or from its text pasted in (from Discord or the league's website, say). You check and correct everything before any rounds are made.
